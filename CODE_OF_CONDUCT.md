# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation welcoming and harassment-free for everyone. Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation for inappropriate behavior. Report incidents privately through the repository contact channel; reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
